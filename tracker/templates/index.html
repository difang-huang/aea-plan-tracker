How to read this

Both scores are percentiles within the registry: 70 means the plan scores above 70% of all registered plans on that dimension, not that it is 70% good. Every score decomposes into named components that are shown on each plan's page, along with the sentences that produced it. Novelty is judged only against plans registered earlier.

Paper links carry a confidence and a method. Links marked declared come from the registry entry itself and are as reliable as the authors' own reporting; rct id citation means the paper's full text cites the trial's registry number; author similarity is inferred and should be checked before use. "Results overdue" is a descriptive publication-bias signal, never a penalty in any score.
